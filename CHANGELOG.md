# Changelog

Changes to the skills in this repository, newest first.

## 27 November 2026

**single-expert-report-review**

Lessons from running the skill on a family consultant's report (Track A):

- New Step 1C: reconcile the date of every alleged incident in the report against the filed documents, including the year.
- Children: read the sentences that record what the child said word by word, and record any that stop part way.
- Order, scope and form: compare the tick boxes on the report's information-sources page with what the body says was done and read.
- Step 1B: record the material cut-off (last filed document supplied, interview dates, report date) and mark the gap "Not assessable".
- Step 1: scanned court forms and affidavits need OCR, and quotations taken from OCR text must be checked against the page.
- Currency: score against the law in force when the report was written, and say where only the current text was checked.
- Errors in the court's order go in a separate note and are not scored against the author.
- New output section: "What the report did properly".
- If final orders have been made, say the within-proceedings routes are spent and list what remains.
- "Not assessable" items are counted separately, outside the severity totals.

Earlier the same day:

- New Step 1B: sort every document supplied into four groups by whether the author had it.
- New rule "Confirm every number": check every section and rule number in the output against the live law before delivering.

**Whole repository**

- Free use widened to anyone involved in family law proceedings, including lawyers and other professionals. Every user is responsible for checking the law a skill cites. See `LICENSE.md` and `DISCLAIMER.md`.
- New illustrated `USER-MANUAL.md`, and instructions for using the skills on other AI tools.

## 26 November 2026

**single-expert-report-review**

- Now covers court report writers as well as psychiatrist single experts: three tracks (family consultants under s 62G and r 8.11; private psychologists and social workers as single experts; other private reports), with a Track A checklist (Domains F1 to F6), severity list and challenge routes.
- New gate check 4: release restriction. A court order limiting who may see a report comes before s 114S(2)(d).
- New Step 1A: reconcile what the expert says was received against the record of what was sent.
- Each defect is counted once. Pinpoints are checked before delivery. No citations beyond those the skill names.
- Part 7.1 of the Rules and rule 8.11 checked against Compilation No. 4. Three wording corrections.
- Family Law Act sections checked. Section 69ZT corrected to section 102NL (in force from 10 June 2025). Three wording corrections.

**Whole repository**

- First public release: five skills, with a disclaimer, licence, scope and provenance files.
