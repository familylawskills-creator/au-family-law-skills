# User guide: Complaint Letter Format

**Skill folder:** `complaint-letter-format`

## What it does
A fixed layout for complaints and submissions to regulators and legal bodies (for example the OLSC, Law Society, Legal Aid, HCCC or AHPRA). Every claim sits next to the quoted document and the exact provision, so a complaints officer can check each sentence without re-reading. Other skills hand their findings to this format.

## When to use it
Use it whenever a review needs to become a letter: a complaint, a supplementary submission, or a response to a regulator.

**Example requests:**
- "Turn this review into a complaint to the Law Society."
- "Rewrite my draft complaint in the standard format."
- "Prepare the enclosure bundle for this complaint."

## What to give it
Upload or paste these. The first one is required; the rest make the result stronger.

1. **The findings** (from another skill, or your own notes).
2. **The documents** you rely on, with page or paragraph numbers.
3. Your contact details and the regulator's reference number, if any.
4. Your last sent letter, if you want the new one to match its style.

If something is missing, the skill marks the affected checks "Not assessable" and asks for it. It asks one question at a time.

## What you get back
- A letter with: sender and addressee blocks, subject, opening paragraph, a three-point scope, Parts 1 to 3 (context, grounds, documents and outcome sought), closing, and Annexures (verbatim extracts and comparison tables).
- Each ground in three steps: **what the document says**, **what the person had** at the time, **breach**.
- Word and PDF files, an enclosure bundle with cover sheets, and a draft cover email.

## How to read the results
Square brackets mark facts only you can confirm. Fill or remove every one before sending. The sending version has no brackets and no drafting notes.

## Tips
- Keep enclosure numbers the same across every body that gets the same material.
- Describe conduct and its effect. Leave out adjectives and guesses about motive; they weaken a complaint.
- Check the reference number against the regulator's own letter, not a web-form ID.

## Works with
- `single-expert-report-review`: expert complaints
- `icl-transcript-review`: ICL complaints
- `party-solicitor-conduct-review`: solicitor complaints

## Before you rely on it
- **Not legal advice.** It checks documents against the law. It does not tell you what to do in your case. Speak to a solicitor, Legal Aid or LawAccess NSW before acting.
- **Check every citation.** The skill tells the AI to verify current law, but AI can still be wrong. Open each section and case it cites.
- **Keep it private.** Section 114Q of the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth) makes it an offence to publish anything that identifies people in family law proceedings. Never post a report online or share it outside your lawyer, the court or a regulator.
