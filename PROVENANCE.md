# Provenance

Where the legal content in these skills came from, when it was last checked, and how far you can rely on it.

## How the skills were written

- Written by a self-represented person, with the help of AI, while working through their own family law matter.
- Built from the official sources listed below: legislation, court rules, court publications and professional codes.
- **Not written, reviewed or approved by a lawyer.**

## Verification status

**Read this before relying on any citation.**

- The dates below are the dates recorded inside each skill when its sources were checked against the official text. Those checks were AI-assisted.
- No person has independently confirmed every section number, rule number, clause or case citation.
- The skills cite about 20 court and tribunal decisions. **None has been independently verified.** AI tools sometimes invent cases or attach the wrong point to a real one.
- Each skill tells the AI to re-check that the law is current before it answers. That check needs web search turned on, and it can still miss a change.

## How to check a citation yourself

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Commonwealth Acts, Rules and Regulations | legislation.gov.au |
| NSW Acts and Regulations | legislation.nsw.gov.au |
| Court and tribunal decisions | austlii.edu.au, or the court's own judgments page |
| Court forms, practice directions and guidelines | fcfcoa.gov.au |
| Professional codes | The website of the body that issued the code |

Search for the Act or rule by name, open the current version, and read the section yourself. For a case, search the citation exactly as written. If you cannot find it, do not rely on it.

## Found an error?

Open an issue on this repository titled "Citation error". Say which skill and which citation, and what the official source says. Do not put personal or case details in the issue.
