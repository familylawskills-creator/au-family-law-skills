# User guide: ICL Conduct Review

**Skill folder:** `icl-transcript-review`

## What it does
Reviews an Independent Children's Lawyer's (ICL) conduct against section 68LA of the Family Law Act 1975, the ICL Guidelines (2024), the FCFCOA Rules and the barristers' or solicitors' conduct rules. It works from a transcript, judgment or the ICL's own documents, and produces a balanced findings report.

## When to use it
Use it when you believe the ICL was not impartial, did not meet the children, did not test evidence, or misled the court, and you want material for a Legal Aid or OLSC complaint, a costs application or an appeal.

**Example requests:**
- "Check what the ICL did at the hearing on this transcript."
- "Was the ICL impartial in these submissions?"
- "List possible complaint grounds against the ICL."

## What to give it
Upload or paste these. The first one is required; the rest make the result stronger.

1. **A primary source** (required): the hearing transcript, the judgment, the ICL's filed documents or correspondence. The skill will not work from your description alone.
2. The ICL's name, and whether a barrister appeared for them.
3. Any subpoena registers or letters from the ICL.

If something is missing, the skill marks the affected checks "Not assessable" and asks for it. It asks one question at a time.

## What you get back
- A report with: matter context, a summary verdict per area, a findings table (one row per duty), the three to five strongest findings with quotes, gaps and next steps, and sources.

## How to read the results
Each duty is marked **Compliant**, **Breach**, **Concern** or **Not observable**. "Not observable" means the document cannot show it (for example whether the ICL met the child). The gaps section tells you what document would answer it.

## Tips
- The skill confirms who the ICL is and whether they are a solicitor or barrister first, because different conduct rules apply.
- Where counsel appeared for the ICL, in-court conduct is counsel's and out-of-court duties are the ICL's. The report keeps these apart.
- A balanced report, which says where the ICL complied, is more persuasive to a regulator.

## Works with
- `complaint-letter-format`: to turn findings into a complaint
- `party-solicitor-conduct-review`: for a party's own solicitor rather than the ICL

## Before you rely on it
- **Not legal advice.** It checks documents against the law. It does not tell you what to do in your case. Speak to a solicitor, Legal Aid or LawAccess NSW before acting.
- **Check every citation.** The skill tells the AI to verify current law, but AI can still be wrong. Open each section and case it cites.
- **Keep it private.** Section 114Q of the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth) makes it an offence to publish anything that identifies people in family law proceedings. Never post a report online or share it outside your lawyer, the court or a regulator.
