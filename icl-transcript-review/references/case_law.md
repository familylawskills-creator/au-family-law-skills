# Case law on ICL duties — starting points

Verify each citation and check for anything newer before relying on it: search the FCFCOA judgments page (fcfcoa.gov.au/judgments) and AustLII for "68LA" plus the sub-issue. Appellate authority (FedCFamC1A) outranks first-instance (FedCFamC1F / FedCFamC2F).

## Role and independence
- ***Re K* (1994) 17 Fam LR 537; (1994) FLC 92-461** — Full Court. The foundational statement of when an ICL should be appointed (the categories reproduced in Guidelines 3.1) and of the ICL as a best-interests advocate who forms an independent view on the evidence.
- ***P & P* (1995) FLC 92-615** — Full Court. The ICL's role includes testing the evidence and putting a positive case; not a passive observer.
- ***Bondelmonte v Bondelmonte* [2017] HCA 8** — High Court. Weight to be given to children's views is for the court; views are a consideration, not determinative; the court is not obliged to accept a child's stated preference where the evidence shows it is the product of influence or not in their interests. Use when the ICL departed from views (Guidelines 7.10.4) or when the ICL overstated the weight of views.

## Meeting the child — s 68LA(5A)–(5D) (post-6 May 2024)
- ***Tandy & Padula* [2024] FedCFamC2F 671** (Judge Beckhouse, 29 May 2024) — one of the first decisions on the new duty. Father alleged the child refused to meet the ICL; court held it has power to order a parent to facilitate the meeting (s 68L(2)(b); s 68LA(5AA)) and made such orders. Use where a parent obstructed the meeting and the ICL did (or did not) seek facilitation orders (Guidelines 7.4.4).
- First-instance decisions have made final orders despite no meeting where the child was under 5 (s 68LA(5B)(a)) — the exception is age-based and needs no court determination; contrast "exceptional circumstances", which does (s 68LA(5D)).
- **Appellate authority on s 68LA(5D)**: search "68LA(5D)" FedCFamC1A for any decision treating a failure to make the (5D) determination before final orders as appealable error. As at the baseline none had been identified; treat the point as open and argue from the mandatory language ("the court must").

## Discharge and costs against or in favour of the ICL
- ***Winston & Winston* [2026] FedCFamC1A 88** (18 May 2026) — ICL sought discharge where the mother's conduct prevented the ICL from discharging its statutory function; application for each parent to contribute to the ICL's costs — father's contribution dismissed, mother ordered to pay from her s 79 entitlement. Use for: (a) the proposition that an ICL who cannot perform the role should apply to be discharged rather than continue; (b) the costs consequences of obstructing an ICL; (c) the court's assessment of what "unable to discharge the statutory function" looks like.
- **s 117 FLA** and the r 3.11 costs statement govern any application for parties to contribute.

## Reports and the s 68LA(5)(c) duty
- Decisions criticising an ICL for adopting a family report wholesale, or for failing to draw adverse content to the court's attention, are mostly first-instance and fact-specific. Search "68LA(5)(c)" and "independent children's lawyer" + "report" + "drawn to the court's attention".

## Procedural fairness and options beyond those the parties put
- Full Court (2025–2026 case notes, e.g. *Provenza (No 4)* [2025] FedCFamC1A 232 line of authority on parenting injunctions; and the March 2026 FLPA case notes): a primary judge may in rare cases need to consider options beyond those put by the parties, but such cases are rare *especially where there is an ICL* — because the ICL is expected to have put the best-interests option. Use to show that the ICL's failure to put an obvious alternative deprived the court of the option.

## Family violence and s 102NA
- ***Ramsey & Ramsey* [2019] FamCAFC 184** and subsequent authority on the mandatory operation of s 102NA. Use where the ICL did not raise the bar on direct cross-examination.

## Children's views — age and weight
- ***R & R: Children's Wishes* (2000) FLC 93-000** — older children's views carry significant weight; ***Harrison & Woollard* (1995) FLC 92-598** — weight depends on maturity and the basis of the view. Pair with *Bondelmonte*.
