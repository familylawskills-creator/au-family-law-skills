## Style rules

- Quote; don't paraphrase the ICL. Page/line every quote.
- One classification per row. If torn between Breach and Concern, choose Concern and explain in the note what would elevate it.
- Record compliance explicitly — "the ICL correctly raised s 102NA at T 3.5" — it is evidence of balance and it narrows the complaint to what matters.
- No speculation about motive. Describe conduct and its effect.
- Numbers in the summary table must match the rows in §3.
