# ICL Compliance Framework — obligations matrix

Work through every domain. For each row record: transcript/document citation → classification (Compliant / Breach / Concern / Not observable) → note. "Obs." tells you where the evidence usually lives: **T** = directly observable in a transcript; **R** = usually only on the record if someone refers to it; **D** = needs the ICL's documents, the court file or the Legal Aid file.

Legend for sources: **FLA** = Family Law Act 1975 (Cth); **G** = Guidelines for Independent Children's Lawyers (updated 2024); **R** = FCFCOA (Family Law) Rules 2021 (Compilation No. 4, 10 June 2025); **BR** = Legal Profession Uniform Conduct (Barristers) Rules 2015; **ASCR** = Legal Profession Uniform Law Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules 2015 (as amended 1 July 2026).

## Domain 1 — Independence and the best-interests role

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Form an independent view, based on the evidence available, of what is in the child's best interests | FLA s 68LA(2)(a); G 4.1–4.2 | T/R | Adopts a party's position without stating an independent analysis; "I'm content to abide" on a contested best-interests question |
| 1.2 | Act in the proceedings in what the ICL believes to be the child's best interests | FLA s 68LA(2)(b) | T | Submissions driven by convenience, cost, or a party's preference rather than the evidence |
| 1.3 | If satisfied a course of action is in the child's best interests, make a submission suggesting it | FLA s 68LA(3); G 7.9 | T | Declines to put a position ("neutral") where the evidence plainly supports one; fails to propose orders at all |
| 1.4 | Not the child's legal representative; not bound by the child's instructions — but must say so transparently | FLA s 68LA(4); G 4.3 | T | Presents the child's wishes as the ICL's position, or vice versa, without distinguishing them |
| 1.5 | Independent of the court, the parties and the Legal Aid commission's funding interests | G 4.4; G 6.3 | T/R | Acts as a de facto second counsel for one party; defers to the judge's apparent preference rather than the evidence |

## Domain 2 — Impartiality in dealings with the parties

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Act impartially in dealings with the parties | FLA s 68LA(5)(a); G 6.3 | T | Hostile or dismissive language toward one party; tests only one party's evidence in cross-examination; characterises one party's conduct using the other party's labels |
| 2.2 | Even-handed communication: both parties (or their lawyers) kept informed of ICL steps (subpoenas, reports, proposals) | G 6.3, 6.4; R 2.27 | R/D | One party learns of an ICL proposal only at the bar table; correspondence sent to one side only |
| 2.3 | Not take instructions from, or run the case of, either party | G 4.4 | T | "I'm instructed that…" from a party; adopting a party's affidavit characterisations verbatim |
| 2.4 | Caution with a self-represented litigant — explain role, do not exploit lack of representation | G 6.5; BR 55 / ASCR 29 (dealing with other parties) | T | Taking advantage of an SRL's procedural errors to advance a position; failing to explain ICL role when asked |

## Domain 3 — The child's views and participation

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Ensure any views expressed by the child on matters in the proceedings are **fully** put before the court | FLA s 68LA(5)(b); G 7.10 | T | Views summarised selectively, diluted ("the child has some ambivalence"), omitted, or mentioned only when consistent with the ICL's position |
| 3.2 | Meet with the child (unless under 5, child does not want to, or exceptional circumstances) | FLA s 68LA(5A)(a), (5B); G 7.4 | R/D | No reference to a meeting at any stage; judge or counsel notes the ICL has not met the child |
| 3.3 | Provide the child the opportunity to express views (same exceptions) | FLA s 68LA(5A)(b), (5B); G 7.4; FLA s 60CE (child cannot be compelled) | R/D | Views obtained only second-hand through a report writer; child never offered a direct opportunity |
| 3.4 | Timing, frequency and method of meeting are the ICL's professional judgment, but must occur before final orders | FLA s 68LA(5AA); G 7.4.1 | R/D | Meeting left so late that views are stale at trial; meeting only after evidence closed |
| 3.5 | If relying on "exceptional circumstances", put that to the court; the court must determine it before final orders and, if not satisfied, order the meeting | FLA s 68LA(5B)(c), (5C), (5D); G 7.4.2–7.4.3 | T | Final orders made with no meeting and no s 68LA(5D) determination on the record |
| 3.6 | Where the ICL's submission departs from the child's views, explain the departure to the court and, age-appropriately, to the child | G 7.10.3–7.10.4; FLA s 60CC(2)(b) | T | Submits for an outcome contrary to the child's stated view without acknowledging the view or giving reasons |
| 3.7 | Confidentiality: not obliged to disclose, and cannot be required to disclose, what the child said — but may disclose if in the child's best interests, even against the child's wishes | FLA s 68LA(6)–(8); G 7.7 | T | Discloses the child's communications gratuitously; or withholds information plainly necessary for safety |
| 3.8 | Explain to the child the role, process, limits of confidentiality, and ultimately the outcome, in an age-appropriate way | G 5, 7.3, 7.13 | D | (Rarely visible on transcript — list under gaps) |
| 3.9 | Consider whether the child should give evidence / be interviewed by the judge; advise the court on the appropriate mechanism (r 8.10) | G 7.10.5; R 8.10 | T | Child's views put through an inappropriate mechanism, or not at all, with no submission on alternatives |

## Domain 4 — Evidence, reports and expert material

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | For any report or document relating to the child used in the proceedings: analyse it to identify the matters the ICL considers most significant to best interests, **and** ensure those matters are properly drawn to the court's attention | FLA s 68LA(5)(c)(i)–(ii); G 7.6 | T | Report recommendations adopted wholesale without analysis; adverse or inconsistent findings in a report not mentioned; methodological problems in a report not raised |
| 4.2 | Arrange for, and manage, independent expert evidence (family report, Child Impact Report, single expert) and instruct the expert even-handedly | G 7.6.1–7.6.3; R 7.03–7.20 (single expert) | R/D | Expert brief drafted from one party's narrative; ICL not the instructing party where it should be |
| 4.3 | Gather and tender relevant evidence from third parties (schools, police, child protection, health) — issue subpoenas; the ICL has unrestricted capacity to subpoena documents at interlocutory hearings | G 7.5, 7.11; R 6.27(2)–(4) | R/D | Material party counsel wanted but ICL did not seek; interim hearing proceeds without obviously available records |
| 4.4 | Test the evidence where necessary — cross-examine parties and witnesses on matters bearing on best interests | G 7.9.2 | T | Declines to cross-examine on a central risk issue; cross-examines only one side |
| 4.5 | Disclosure as a party: duty of disclosure applies; produce documents the ICL relies on | R 3.11(3)(c); R 6.01 | D | Material in the ICL's possession first disclosed at the hearing |
| 4.6 | Communicate with the single expert in accordance with the Rules (ICL may do so; parties' communications are restricted) | R 7.17; R 7.18(2) | R | Private communication with the expert not disclosed to parties |

## Domain 5 — Family violence, risk and safety

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Identify and bring to the court's attention risk of harm, family violence and child abuse issues; ensure the court has the evidence it needs for the s 60CC(2)(a) safety consideration | G 7.5; FLA s 60CC(2)(a), (2A); s 67ZBB | T | Risk allegations not addressed in submissions; s 67ZBB response not sought |
| 5.2 | Raise s 102NA (ban on direct cross-examination) where applicable and ensure the Commonwealth Family Violence and Cross-examination of Parties Scheme is engaged | FLA s 102NA; G 7.5.3 | T | Self-represented alleged perpetrator cross-examines the other party without the ICL raising the bar |
| 5.3 | Endeavour to minimise trauma to the child from the proceedings | FLA s 68LA(5)(d); G 7.3 | T/R | Supports unnecessary further assessments of the child; supports listing practices that prolong uncertainty without reason |
| 5.4 | Consider interim safety and propose interim protective arrangements where warranted | G 7.11 | T | No interim position on supervision/handover safety despite evidence of risk |

## Domain 6 — Settlement, case management and the overarching purpose

| # | Obligation | Source | Obs. | What breach looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Facilitate an agreed resolution to the extent it is in the child's best interests | FLA s 68LA(5)(e); G 7.12 | T/R | Blocks or ignores resolution avenues without a best-interests reason; or pushes settlement that disregards risk |
| 6.2 | Where a proposed consent outcome significantly questions the child's best interests, bring that to the court's attention rather than simply consenting | G 3.1(h), 7.12.2 | T | "The ICL consents" to orders the ICL's own evidence base contradicts, with no explanation |
| 6.3 | Act consistently with the overarching purpose — just, efficient, timely, cost-effective, proportionate | R 1.04; FCFCOA Act 2021 s 67 | T | Unnecessary adjournment applications; late filing; duplicative evidence |
| 6.4 | Prepare a case plan; attend and participate at all court events; comply with directions and timetables | G 7.2; R 3.11(3)(b) | T/D | Non-attendance; unexplained non-compliance with directions; no position at a mention |
| 6.5 | Undertakings proposed by the ICL must comply with r 10.18 (filing/form) | R 10.18 | T/D | Undertaking announced orally and never filed |
| 6.6 | In the Evatt List, follow the Evatt List ICL Guide (early engagement, intensive case management, risk focus) | FCFCOA Guide for ICLs in the Evatt List (10 June 2025) | T/R | Evatt matter handled as a standard list matter |

## Observability guide

| Usually visible on transcript | Usually needs other evidence |
|---|---|
| Submissions, position, consent/non-consent | Whether/when the ICL met the child |
| Cross-examination choices | Case plan, correspondence, Legal Aid file |
| Language toward parties | Subpoena register and returns |
| Whether views were put, and how | Expert briefing letters |
| Whether s 68LA(5D) was addressed | Notice of Address for Service, costs statement |
| Candour, corrections, contrary authority | Communications with the child and the expert |
| Risk/s 102NA raised | Discharge considerations |

Silence on an "other evidence" item is **Not observable**, with a next-step naming the document that would resolve it.
