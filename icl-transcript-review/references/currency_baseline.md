## Verification procedure (run every time)

1. **Guidelines** — `WebSearch: "Guidelines for Independent Children's Lawyers" fcfcoa`. Confirm the page still says "Updated 2024" and no later edition or practice direction on ICLs has been issued. Also glance for any new FCFCOA practice direction mentioning ICLs.
2. **s 68LA** — `WebSearch: Family Law Act 1975 compilation latest legislation.gov.au` and `WebSearch: Family Law Amendment Bill <current year>`. Confirm the latest compilation number/date and that no amending Act has commenced touching s 68L–68LA or Pt VII Div 10.
3. **FCFCOA Rules** — `WebFetch: https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2021L01197/latest/versions`. Confirm Compilation No. 4 (10 June 2025) is still the latest; if a C05 exists, read the amending instrument's explanatory statement for changes to rr 3.11, 2.27, 6.01, 6.27, 8.10, 10.18.
4. **Conduct rules** — `WebSearch: Legal Services Council recent changes Uniform Rules` and, for a solicitor ICL, `WebSearch: Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules amendment <current year>`. Confirm the Barristers Rules are unchanged since 4 March 2022 and the ASCR since 1 July 2026.

Report the outcome in one line if nothing has changed ("Standards verified current as at [date]: FLA C101 (10 Jun 2025); Guidelines 2024; Rules C04 (10 Jun 2025); Barristers Rules 4 Mar 2022; ASCR 1 Jul 2026."). If something has changed, tell the user before analysing, update this file's table, and apply the version in force at the date of the conduct.

## Temporal rules of thumb

- Conduct **before 6 May 2024**: no statutory duty to meet the child; the Guidelines (2013 edition then current) expected it. Do not cite s 68LA(5A).
- Conduct **6 May 2024 – 9 June 2025**: FLA C98–C100; Rules C03.
- Conduct **on or after 10 June 2025**: FLA C101; Rules C04.
- Solicitor conduct **before 1 July 2026**: ASCR 9 Aug 2024 version; after, the 2026 version (duty-to-court rules identical).
