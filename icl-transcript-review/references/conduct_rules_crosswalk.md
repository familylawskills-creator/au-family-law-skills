# Professional conduct rules — Barristers ↔ Solicitors crosswalk (NSW / Uniform Law)

Both sets are made by the Legal Services Council under the Legal Profession Uniform Law (applied in NSW by the Legal Profession Uniform Law Application Act 2014 (NSW); also Vic and, since 2022, WA). Complaints in NSW go to the Office of the Legal Services Commissioner (OLSC), which may refer to the Law Society (solicitors) or the Bar Association (barristers). Queensland, SA, Tasmania, ACT and NT have their own versions of the ASCR / Barristers' Rules with the same substance and (for the ASCR) the same numbering.

## Which set applies

| ICL status | Rules | Current version | Last amended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrister (counsel briefed by the ICL, or a barrister ICL) | Legal Profession Uniform Conduct (Barristers) Rules 2015 (NSW SI 2015 No 243) | 4 March 2022 to date | 2022 (SI 76) — r 123 anti-discrimination/harassment and r 125 definitions. No amendment since (confirmed 27 Oct 2026). Mandatory "Equality and Wellbeing" CPD category added from 1 April 2026 via the CPD (Barristers) Rules, not the Conduct Rules. |
| Solicitor (the ICL on the record; most Legal Aid panel ICLs) | Legal Profession Uniform Law Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules 2015 (NSW SI 2015 No 244) | **1 July 2026 to date** | Legal Profession Uniform Law Australian Solicitors' Conduct Amendment Rules 2026 (SI 2026 No 276), published 19–26 June 2026, commenced 1 July 2026 — amendments to **r 8** (duty to follow instructions: expressly obliges solicitors to explain why they cannot act on unlawful, improper or incompetent instructions) and **r 13** (termination of retainer: clarifies termination where continuing would require breach of ethical duties or statutory obligations, including AML/CTF obligations). Previous version: 9 August 2024 – 30 June 2026. A companion new r 12 in the Legal Profession Uniform Legal Practice (Solicitors) Rules 2015 recommends retainer agreements disclose statutory reporting obligations. |

Conduct before 1 July 2026 by a solicitor is judged under the 9 Aug 2024 version; the 2026 changes do not touch the duty-to-court rules, so for most ICL findings the version makes no difference, but say which one you applied.

## Complaint pathways (NSW)

1. **Legal Aid NSW** — Private Lawyer Quality Standards Unit: breach of the Legal Aid NSW Panel Service Agreement / Quality Standards (which incorporate the ICL Guidelines). Outcome: audit, training, panel removal. Does not require a Uniform Law breach.
2. **OLSC (NSW)** — complaint under the Legal Profession Uniform Law Pt 5.2 for unsatisfactory professional conduct or professional misconduct; must be made within 3 years of the conduct (extendable). Grounds: conduct rules above; s 296–297 Uniform Law definitions.
3. **The court** — raise the conduct in submissions or an application (discharge of the ICL under r 3.11; costs under s 117; appeal ground where the ICL's failure caused a miscarriage, e.g. s 68LA(5D) not addressed before final orders).

Keep the three distinct in the report: a Guidelines shortfall supports (1) and may support (3); only a conduct-rule breach supports (2).
