# Scope

What these skills cover, and what they do not.

## Who they are for

People involved in parenting matters in the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (FCFCOA), whether they represent themselves or are represented by a solicitor, and people helping them without payment.

**If you are involved in a family law matter in any other role:** for example as a family lawyer, solicitor, barrister, Independent Children's Lawyer, expert, report writer, mediator, social worker or support person, you may use the skills in your work free of charge (see `LICENSE.md`, section 2). It is your responsibility to check every Act, rule, regulation, guideline and case a skill cross-references against the current official source before you rely on it. The skills were not written or reviewed by a legal practitioner.

**If you have a solicitor:** use the skills to understand your documents and prepare questions. Take the results to your solicitor before you act on them. Do not send anything to the court, the other party or a regulator without your solicitor's advice.

## What is covered

| Skill | Covers |
|---|---|
| `family-orders-compliance-check` | Whether final parenting orders, property orders or a Binding Child Support Agreement are being followed, using the contravention test in Division 13A of the Family Law Act 1975 |
| `single-expert-report-review` | Single expert reports by a psychiatrist, psychologist or social worker (Part 7.1 of the Rules), and court reports by a Court Child Expert or Panel Family Consultant (Child Impact Report, Specific Issues Report, Family Report) |
| `icl-transcript-review` | The conduct of an Independent Children's Lawyer, from a transcript, judgment or the ICL's own documents |
| `party-solicitor-conduct-review` | The conduct of a solicitor in FCFCOA proceedings: your own, a former one, or the other party's |
| `complaint-letter-format` | The layout of a complaint or submission to a regulator or professional body |

## Jurisdiction

- **Federal law:** the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth), the FCFCOA (Family Law) Rules 2021 and the child support Acts apply across Australia, except that Western Australia has its own Family Court and rules.
- **New South Wales only:** the solicitor conduct, costs and complaint material (Legal Profession Uniform Law (NSW), OLSC, Law Society of NSW, Legal Aid NSW) and the health complaint routes (HCCC, NSW councils). The Uniform Law also applies in Victoria and Western Australia, but the complaint bodies named here are the NSW ones.
- **Other states and territories:** the federal parts still apply. The complaint bodies and some conduct rules are different. Check your own state's regulator.

## What is not covered

- Property settlement, spousal maintenance and binding financial agreements
- Child support assessments, Centrelink and family assistance payments
- Divorce applications
- Family violence orders and criminal proceedings
- Appeals and reviews of a judge's decision
- Child protection (care) proceedings in state children's courts
- Advice on what orders to seek, or on the merits of your case

## What the skills do not do

- They do not give legal advice or predict what a court will do.
- They do not decide who is telling the truth.
- They do not replace reading the order, rule or report yourself.
- They do not file or send anything for you.

## If you are not safe

These skills are for reviewing documents. They are not for emergencies. If you or a child are in danger, call 000. For family violence support, call 1800RESPECT on 1800 737 732.
